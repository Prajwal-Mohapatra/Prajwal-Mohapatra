## About Me

B.Tech student in **Robotics & AI Engineering** at OUTR (CGPA: 9.16), working at the intersection of physical robotics and applied deep learning. I build systems where robots perceive, decide, and act — from drone navigation in simulation to robotic grasping and disaster-prediction ML.

- **Robotics:** ROS2 pipelines, drone simulation & control, robot perception, sensor fusion, 6-DoF ROV design
- **ML/AI:** Medical image segmentation, geospatial fire-risk prediction, robot grasp detection
- **2 peer-reviewed publications**
- **Design Patent filed** — Hybrid ROV hull *(Application Pending)*
- **ML Lead** @ Zairza (Robo-Sof Club), OUTR
